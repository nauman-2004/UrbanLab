● HYPOTHESIS SUPPORTED · WITH A TWIST
Pre-1980 Houston offices devote 3.4× more of their lot to surface parking, but once off-site garages are counted, total parking supply is about the same. Older buildings spread their parking across the ground, while newer ones stack it in garages.

Self-Critique & Evidence
Evidence trail

Lots and build years come from CoStar ×12. Surface parking is segmented from Vexcel 2025-06 ×12. Garages are counted in Matterport ×9. Off-site spaces come from interviews ×3, each with consent and a recording.

Method
01Sampled 12 Class A/B CBD offices (6 / 6 by era)
02Segmented lots with a vision model and spot-checked 3
03Ran Spearman's rank correlation and a Mann-Whitney U test for supply
Limitations
  • Small pilot (n=12). The CBD only, so it may not hold for suburban offices.
  • 3 buildings have no 3D scan. Their garage levels were inferred from CoStar parking notes.
  • Self-reported off-site counts from 3 property managers, not verified against lease documents.
  • Land value isn't controlled for. Older lots may simply have been cheaper.
Suggested follow-up

Expand to about 60 buildings across Houston and LA, and add assessed land value as a control.
